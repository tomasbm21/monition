Why we started Monition

The business is on the public trail before it has chosen a firm.

A lien, an insolvency filing, regulation still in discussion. Nobody at a firm has time to read all of it, so firms hear about the need after the business has already chosen counsel.

We started Monition to read every public trail, see who is immediately affected, and predict who is next in the fallout with predictive swarms of its agentic technology. It reaches out by routing each one to the partner with the warmest way in, draft included. Your partners reach the business before it has chosen a firm. Monition never sends on your behalf, and every forecast is scored against what actually happened, misses included. Monition is invite-only. If that sounds useful, join the waitlist.
