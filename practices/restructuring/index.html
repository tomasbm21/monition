Signals Monition reads for restructuring

  • Creditor lists in other companies’ cases
  • New charges and security over receivables
  • Late or qualified accounts, going-concern notes
  • One customer making up most of the revenue
  • Statutory demands and winding-up petitions
  • Changes to facilities in lenders’ filings
