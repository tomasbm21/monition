The five steps

Read, predict, trace, reach, introduce.

Each step shows its evidence. Nothing moves to the next one without a reason you can check.

01 · Read

Every public trail, read first.

Monition reads liens and charges, insolvency filings, and regulation in discussion, with court dockets, company registers, property registers and annual accounts. Each record keeps its source, date and reference, and is linked to every company it mentions.

Uses
Public records only.2
Gives you
A feed of records, each tied to the companies it touches.
Who decides
No one needs to. Nothing leaves Monition at this step.

02 · Predict

Who is next in the fallout.

Predictive swarms of Monition’s agentic technology read the trails together: a customer that fails, a lender that takes new security, accounts that arrive late, a rule still in discussion. They name the matter, the practice and when it is likely to arrive. Every forecast shows its reasons, so a partner can check it in a minute.

02 · Identify

Who is immediately affected by the change.

A lien recorded, an insolvency filing, a rule in discussion that names a brand. Monition reads who the change reaches now, which practice it falls to, and how strong the evidence is.

03 · Trace

One filing reaches many companies. Monition follows it.

An insolvency filing reaches landlords, suppliers, lenders and buyers. A rule in discussion reaches producers and retailers. Monition maps who is immediately affected, and who is next.

Uses
Creditor lists, charges, leases, producer registers and the links between companies.
Gives you
A map of who is affected, by role, with the day each one appeared.
Who decides
Nothing is sent. The map is for your team.

04 · Reach

The partner with the warmest way in.

Monition reads your CRM, read-only, and routes each company to the partner with the warmest way in: someone they know there, or someone who knows someone. The draft is included. If the company is already a client, the relationship partner gets an alert instead.

DO1stKnows the owner's asset manager

Your CRM, read-only. Never pooled with another firm, never used for training.
Gives you
The partner, the contact and how they know each other.
Who decides
Your conflicts team, in your own system, before anything is drafted.

05 · Introduce

Draft included. The partner sends.

Monition drafts a short note that cites the public records, never the forecast. The partner rewrites what they like and sends it from their own email. Monition never sends anything.

Awaiting partner approval
Uses

Who decides
The partner. Nothing goes out without them.

Getting started

What Monition needs from your firm, and what it never asks for.

Setup is a working session with your business development and risk teams, not an integration project.2

What it needs

  • The practices to watch, and any companies or sectors to watch closely
  • Read-only access to your CRM, or a spreadsheet of relationships
  • A named partner for each practice you want watched
  • Your conflicts team’s usual process. Monition waits for it
  • A short weekly review with each of those partners

What it never asks for

  • Client files or privileged documents
  • Access to anyone’s email. Partners send from their own
  • Data about individuals. Monition covers organisations only
  • A fee per introduction. It is a flat fee per firm4

Questions

What partners and risk teams ask first.

Where does Monition get its data?
From public records only: court dockets, company and charges registers, property registers, annual accounts and regulator notices. Your CRM is read, never written to, and only to find which partner has a way in.
How is a forecast different from a record?
A record already names the company. A forecast says a company is likely to need counsel before any record names it, and shows the signals behind that. Every screen labels which one you are looking at.
Can a draft mention the forecast?
No. Drafts cite public records only. The forecast stays inside your firm.
What if the company is already our client?
The relationship partner gets an alert about the record. The company is never approached as new business.
Does Monition send anything?
No. A partner edits, signs and sends every note from their own email. Monition has no way to send on its own.
Does it cover individuals?
No. Organisations only. Individuals, consumer debtors and sole traders are excluded before anything reaches your team.
How will we know the forecasts are any good?
Every forecast is scored against what later appears in the public record, misses included, in a week-8 scorecard for each firm. How scoring works
What happens to our data?
It stays yours: read-only access, never pooled with another firm’s, never used for training. Data and security
