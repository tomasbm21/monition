Professional conduct

Seven rules, built into the product.

They are written into every firm’s agreement as well. A screen that would break one of them does not exist.
  1. 01Organisations only

    Monition reads about companies. Individuals, consumer debtors and sole traders are excluded before anything is forecast.

    Where you see it: Applied when records are read
  2. 02Conflicts cleared first

    Every company goes to your conflicts team and is cleared in your own system before a word is drafted. A blocked company stops there.

    Where you see it: Conflicts queue
  3. 03The partner sends, never Monition

    A partner edits, signs and sends from their own email. Monition has no way to send anything to anyone.

    Where you see it: Introduce
  4. 04Clients are alerted, never approached

    When a filing touches an existing client, the relationship partner gets an alert. Monition never suggests approaching a company that may be adverse to a client.

    Where you see it: Client alert
  5. 05Drafts cite the record, never the prediction

    A draft refers only to public records the recipient can read for themselves. It never mentions a forecast.

    Where you see it: Introduce
  6. 06Your data stays yours

    Read-only access to your CRM. Your data is never pooled with another firm and never used to train a model.2

    Where you see it: Onboarding
  7. 07A flat fee per firm

    Never a fee per company, per introduction or per matter, so no one is paid for a referral.3

    Where you see it: Each firm’s agreement

What we never do

The list that does not change.

If a firm asks for any of these, the answer is no. Monition will never:
  • Send an email, letter or message on anyone’s behalf
  • Contact a company, or anyone who works at one
  • Ask for access to a partner’s inbox or calendar
  • Forecast anything about individuals, consumers or sole traders
  • Suggest approaching a company that may be adverse to a client
  • Mention a forecast in a draft
  • Pool one firm’s data with another’s, or train a model on it
  • Charge per company, per introduction or per matter
  • Change or delete anything in your CRM
  • Claim a certificate it does not hold

  1. Does Monition contact anyone on the firm’s behalf?

    No. Monition prepares a first draft for a partner. The partner edits it, signs it and sends it from their own email, or decides not to. Monition has no sending capability.

  2. Who does Monition forecast about?

    Organisations only. Individuals, consumer debtors and sole traders are excluded when records are read, before any forecast is made.

  3. How are conflicts handled?

    Before anything is drafted, the company is passed to the firm’s conflicts team and cleared in the firm’s own system. Monition records the result as pending, cleared or blocked. A blocked company goes no further.

  4. What happens when a filing touches one of our clients?

    The relationship partner receives an alert. The client is never treated as a company to approach, and Monition never suggests approaching a company that may be adverse to a client.

  5. What does a draft say about how the firm found the company?

    Only what the public record says. A draft cites records the recipient can read for themselves. It never mentions a forecast or Monition.

  6. What access to our systems does Monition need?

    Read-only access to the CRM, to see which partner already knows someone at a company, and a client list for alerts. It never asks for mailbox, calendar or document access, and it cannot change anything in the CRM.

    Scopes per CRM: [to confirm per system]

  7. Is our data shared with other firms or used to train models?

    Never. A firm’s data is never pooled with another firm’s and never used to train a model, ours or a provider’s.

  8. Where is data hosted, and for how long is it kept?

    Hosting region: [to confirm]. Retention: [term + period to confirm]. When a firm leaves, its data is deleted within [period to confirm] and deletion confirmed in writing.

  9. Who processes our data besides Monition?

    The hosting and model providers are agreed with each firm in writing before it starts, and a firm is told before one changes.

  10. How is Monition paid?

    A flat fee per firm. Never per company, per introduction or per matter, so no one is paid for a referral. Pricing is agreed with each firm.

  11. What security certifications does Monition hold?

    Not yet. The security roadmap on this page lists what is planned and when, and nothing on it is claimed until it is held.

  12. How will we know whether the forecasts are any good?

    Every forecast is scored against what later appears in the public record, misses first. Week 8 for every firm is a scorecard. How scoring works.

  13. Is Monition giving legal advice?

    No. Monition is not a law firm and gives no legal advice. It shows partners what the public record says and who they already know.
